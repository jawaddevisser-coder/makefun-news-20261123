Entry for the [make.fun news-image-20261123 bounty](https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261123): one image summing up the world's news of Monday 23 November 2026.

**Style:** "The World Tonight" — an evening bulletin poster: deep navy, gold rules, a red lead banner, and six story cards with simple pictograms drawn from scratch. A new look, distinct from our earlier news entries (cream newspaper, dark broadcast, white magazine, telex).

**Program:** `main.ts` (Node 22, standard library only) copies `entry.png` to `/output/entry.png`. The image is drawn deterministically by `draw_news.py` (Pillow) — run it twice and the PNG bytes are identical.

## The six stories (all happened or first reported on Monday 23 November 2026)

1. **Ukraine — 15 killed in overnight strikes.** Russian drones hit homes in the Kyiv region; a block of flats collapses in Dymer, killing 11, as rescuers dig for survivors. Sources: Al Jazeera, Reuters.
2. **Libya — Kyiv says it sank a Russian warship off Libya.** Zelenskyy says seven sea drones sank the corvette Merkury at Benghazi. Sources: Reuters, CNN, AP.
3. **South Korea — exports more than double.** Nov 1–20 exports hit a record $77.4bn, up 101% on a year ago, as chip shipments nearly quadruple. Source: Korea Customs Service via Korea JoongAng Daily.
4. **Markets — Wall Street rebounds after AI selloff.** S&P futures jump 2.2% and Nasdaq 3% early Monday after last week's tech rout. Source: Reuters.
5. **India — Modi and Nepal's PM unveil train.** Balen Shah gets a ceremonial welcome in Delhi; 100-minute talks end with a new Ayodhya–Janakpur train. Sources: IANS, New Kerala.
6. **Australia — Molly Meldrum, 83, dies.** Australian music legend behind Countdown — champion of AC/DC and Kylie Minogue — dies in Melbourne after a long illness. Sources: Reuters, AAP.

**Date discipline:** stories from the weekend were deliberately left out or re-worded to what actually happened on Monday — the Democrats' 258th House seat was won on Sunday 22 Nov, the Common Cents Act was signed on Friday 20 Nov, the Indonesia ship seizure happened on 7 Nov (disclosed 20 Nov), and the Merkury was struck on Sunday 22 Nov (only Zelenskyy's claim of it is Monday's news).
